# Torun Mart Website Redesign Plan

**Status:** Design direction approved for exploration  
**Prepared:** 18 August 2026  
**Reference site:** [torunmart.com](https://torunmart.com/)  
**Primary market:** Bangladesh  
**Primary language:** Bangla, with selective English support

## 1. Project Goal

Redesign Torun Mart as a trustworthy, modern Bangladeshi marketplace that makes it easy to discover and buy authentic food, seasonal produce, books, fashion, and everyday products.

The new experience should feel warmer, clearer, and more credible than the existing template while improving product discovery and reducing the effort required to order—especially on mobile.

### Core outcomes

- Make Torun Mart's identity clear within five seconds.
- Lead with real products, real benefits, and real customer proof.
- Help users find categories and products with fewer taps.
- Make price, discount, delivery, payment, and return information easy to understand.
- Create a reusable visual system that can scale across homepage, catalog, product, cart, and checkout.
- Improve perceived quality without making the store feel expensive or inaccessible.

## 2. Existing Website Audit

### What exists today

The live site uses WordPress, WooCommerce, Elementor, and the Clotya theme. The homepage follows a generic marketplace template: utility header, search, fashion hero, service highlights, product carousels, promotional banners, category slider, reviews, and deal sections.

The underlying commerce features are useful and should be retained where relevant:

- Product search and category browsing
- Account, wishlist, compare, cart, and quick view
- Sale pricing and product variations
- Delivery and seven-day return information
- Customer reviews
- Mobile sticky add-to-cart behavior

### Main problems to solve

1. **Brand and catalog mismatch** — The fashion-led hero and generic template imagery do not represent the strongest real categories: honey, mustard oil, gur, seasonal fruit, books, and mixed essentials.
2. **Broken credibility** — Multiple banner and brand images fail to load; placeholder text such as “Lorem ipsum” remains visible; expired demo offers and template product content appear on the homepage.
3. **Weak hierarchy** — Many equal-weight carousels make the page long and visually repetitive without establishing a clear shopping path.
4. **Inconsistent imagery** — Product photos vary in crop, lighting, background, quality, and aspect ratio. Some imagery appears to be generic stock photography.
5. **Low information clarity** — Important product facts, delivery timing, return conditions, and payment options are scattered or buried in long content blocks.
6. **Mixed language and tone** — Bangla and English are used inconsistently, and some labels do not sound natural for the target audience.
7. **Template-like styling** — Thin type, low-contrast gray copy, small controls, and excess white space weaken usability and distinctiveness.
8. **Mobile friction risk** — Dense product controls, multiple sliders, and small touch targets are likely to make browsing harder on the primary mobile experience.
9. **Unfocused conversion path** — The current homepage does not clearly answer: Why Torun Mart? What should I buy? Can I trust the quality? How quickly will it arrive?
10. **Content and trust inconsistencies** — Contact details vary across indexed pages, and policy content is repeated at excessive length instead of being summarized contextually.

## 3. Brand Positioning

### Proposed promise

**বিশ্বস্ত পণ্য, সহজ কেনাকাটা।**  
Trusted products, made easy to buy.

### Brand attributes

- Trustworthy, not corporate
- Local, not provincial
- Fresh and warm, not rustic or old-fashioned
- Practical, not flashy
- Curated, not cluttered

### Visual concept: “Modern Bangla Market”

Use the warmth and familiarity of a trusted neighborhood market with the order, polish, and confidence of a modern commerce product. Real produce textures, editorial product photography, Bangla-first typography, restrained color, and concise proof points should do most of the visual work.

Avoid generic fashion-template photography, heavy gradients, glass effects, excessive animation, tiny text, and decorative icons without meaning.

## 4. Audience and Priority Journeys

### Primary audiences

- Mobile-first shoppers in Dhaka seeking authentic food and household products
- Customers outside Dhaka who need clear delivery cost and timing
- Seasonal shoppers looking for mangoes, gur, honey, or gifts
- Book buyers looking for trusted titles and curated bundles
- Returning customers who want to reorder quickly

### Priority journeys

1. Homepage → category → product → add to cart → checkout
2. Search → product result → product → checkout
3. Campaign link/social post → product → trust check → order
4. Homepage → seasonal offer → bundle → checkout
5. Account/order history → reorder

## 6. Homepage Blueprint

### 1. Announcement bar

One concise, rotating-free message such as “ঢাকায় ডেলিভারি ২–৩ দিন • ৭ দিনের সহজ রিটার্ন”. Include a visible help link. Avoid an auto-rotating ticker.

### 2. Header

- Clear, simplified logo
- Prominent full-width search with Bangla placeholder
- Category access
- Account and cart with readable labels or tooltips
- Sticky behavior after the hero, with a compact mobile version

### 3. Hero

Use one strong campaign rather than a large slider.

- Real Torun Mart product or seasonal category photography
- Specific Bangla headline and one-sentence benefit
- Primary CTA: **এখনই কিনুন**
- Secondary CTA: **সব ক্যাটাগরি দেখুন**
- Optional proof badge: authentic sourcing, cash on delivery, or delivery timeline

### 4. Trust strip

Four concise points with consistent line icons:

- যাচাইকৃত মান
- ক্যাশ অন ডেলিভারি
- দ্রুত ডেলিভারি
- ৭ দিনের সহজ রিটার্ন

### 5. Shop by category

Six to eight photo-led category cards. Order dynamically by season and revenue rather than alphabetically. Each card needs a short Bangla label and consistent imagery.

### 6. Best sellers

Show four to eight genuinely popular items. Avoid a carousel on desktop; use horizontal scroll only on mobile. Include image, name, unit/variant, current price, previous price when relevant, discount, rating count, and one obvious add-to-cart action.

### 7. Seasonal campaign

A full-width editorial block for the current high-intent category—for example mango season, winter gur, honey collection, Ramadan gifts, or book fair picks. Content and artwork must be manageable through the CMS.

### 8. Why Torun Mart

Explain sourcing and quality control through three short proof cards and one real behind-the-scenes image. Replace vague slogans with verifiable facts.

### 9. Curated collections

Two or three commerce-led blocks such as “খাঁটি খাবার”, “বইয়ের জনপ্রিয় কম্বো”, and “৳১,০০০-এর মধ্যে”. These should link to filtered listings.

### 10. Customer proof

Use three verified reviews with customer name, purchased product, rating, and date. Add user photos only with permission. Remove anonymous template testimonials.

### 11. Reassurance and support

A compact delivery/return explainer plus WhatsApp or phone assistance. Link to full policies instead of reproducing long policy text on every page.

### 12. Footer

- Category and support links
- Accurate address, phone, email, and business hours
- Payment and delivery partners
- Social links
- Newsletter or offer opt-in only if there is a real communication program
- Copyright and legal links

## 7. Product Listing and Search

- Use a responsive 2-column mobile, 3-column tablet, and 4-column desktop grid.
- Keep product card heights consistent and names to two lines.
- Offer filters relevant to each category: price, availability, author/publisher, food type, size/weight, and discount.
- Use a mobile bottom sheet for filters and sorting.
- Default sorting should favor relevance or popularity, not arbitrary recency.
- Provide useful empty states with category suggestions and support.
- Search should tolerate Bangla/English spelling variants and common transliterations where technically feasible.
- Do not expose wishlist, compare, quick view, and add-to-cart at equal visual priority. Add-to-cart is primary; wishlist is secondary; compare should appear only where it adds value.

## 8. Product Detail Page

### Above the fold

- Consistent image gallery with zoom
- Category breadcrumb
- Product name and concise value statement
- Rating and review count
- Price, savings, stock, and unit/weight
- Variation selector with clear selected state
- Quantity and strong add-to-cart button
- Delivery estimate by location
- Cash-on-delivery and return reassurance

### Below the fold

- Short benefits or highlights
- Description with scannable headings
- Specifications, ingredients/materials, origin, and care/storage as applicable
- Delivery and return summary in accordions
- Verified reviews
- Related and frequently bought-together products

On mobile, use a sticky purchase bar that never hides required options or obstructs content.

## 9. Cart and Checkout

- Prefer a cart drawer for quick confirmation, with a clear path to checkout.
- Show product thumbnail, variant, quantity, subtotal, delivery estimate, and easy remove/undo.
- Use a short guest checkout; account creation must be optional.
- Prioritize Bangladesh-friendly phone and address fields.
- Reveal shipping cost before final submission whenever possible.
- Support cash on delivery and available local payment methods with clear status feedback.
- Keep coupon entry secondary so it does not distract users without a code.
- Show order summary persistently on desktop and accessibly on mobile.
- Confirmation must include order number, item summary, delivery expectation, support route, and tracking action.

## 10. Visual Design System

### Color direction

Final values should be accessibility-tested during UI design.

| Token | Suggested value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | `#164B35` | Brand, primary buttons, header accents |
| Leaf | `#2F7A52` | Secondary actions and positive states |
| Saffron | `#E8A11A` | Offers, highlights, rating stars |
| Terracotta | `#C55232` | Urgent sale or limited-stock accents |
| Warm canvas | `#FAF7F0` | Page background and editorial sections |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and raised surfaces |
| Ink | `#17211B` | Primary text |
| Muted ink | `#667069` | Secondary text |
| Border | `#E2E5DF` | Dividers and input boundaries |

Do not rely on red or green alone to communicate status.

### Typography

- Bangla: **Noto Sans Bengali** or **Hind Siliguri**
- Latin/numbers: **Inter** or the matching Latin forms from the selected family
- Base body: 16px minimum, 1.55–1.7 line height
- Use no more than four functional weights
- Prefer sentence case; avoid all-caps navigation and overly thin headings

### Layout and shape

- Max content width: 1200–1280px
- 12-column desktop grid, 8-column tablet, 4-column mobile
- 8px spacing foundation with a restrained scale
- Card radius: 12–16px; button/input radius: 8–12px
- Subtle 1px borders; shadows only when needed to communicate elevation
- Minimum touch target: 44 × 44px

### Photography

- Use real products and real sourcing environments.
- Standardize product thumbnails to a 4:5 aspect ratio.
- Use a warm neutral background and consistent lighting.
- Avoid text baked into product images.
- Define separate art direction for food, books, fashion, and seasonal campaigns while keeping color treatment consistent.

## 11. Content and Language Rules

- Default to Bangla for customer-facing commerce content; use English for established product names where it helps recognition.
- Use one approved spelling for the brand: **Torun Mart / তরুণ মার্ট**.
- Replace generic claims with proof: source, weight, ingredients, warranty, return window, or delivery time.
- Keep headings benefit-led and body copy short.
- Standardize price as `৳১,২৪৫` or `১,২৪৫৳` across the entire product; do not mix formats.
- Maintain a single source of truth for phone, email, address, policies, and delivery charges.
- Remove placeholder copy, expired demo campaigns, fake inventory pressure, and unverified health claims.

## 12. Interaction and Motion

- Use 150–250ms transitions for hover, focus, drawer, and add-to-cart feedback.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Avoid auto-rotating hero sliders and review carousels.
- Provide immediate loading, success, error, and out-of-stock states.
- Preserve scroll position when closing quick views or filter sheets.
- Confirm add-to-cart without unexpectedly navigating away.

## 13. Accessibility, Performance, and SEO

### Accessibility

- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA color contrast and keyboard behavior.
- Use visible focus states and semantic heading order.
- Provide useful Bangla alt text for meaningful images.
- Associate every form control with a label and actionable error message.
- Ensure dialogs, sheets, menus, and carousels work with keyboard and screen readers.

### Performance

- Target Core Web Vitals: LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms, CLS ≤ 0.1 at the 75th percentile.
- Serve responsive AVIF/WebP images with explicit dimensions.
- Preload only the hero image and essential font files.
- Remove unused theme/plugin assets and avoid stacking multiple slider libraries.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold imagery and third-party widgets.

### SEO and commerce metadata

- Use unique Bangla-first titles and descriptions.
- Add valid Product, Offer, AggregateRating, BreadcrumbList, and Organization structured data where supported by real content.
- Maintain crawlable category pages, canonical URLs, and descriptive product slugs.
- Keep product availability, price, and review data synchronized with the storefront.

## 14. Responsive Behavior

### Mobile: 320–767px

- One primary task per row; no compressed desktop header.
- Two-column product grid with readable names and prices.
- Horizontal category chips or cards with clear scroll affordance.
- Bottom-sheet filters and sticky purchase/navigation controls.
- Hide low-value secondary actions until requested.

### Tablet: 768–1023px

- Three-column product grid.
- Compact header with full search.
- Two-column editorial modules.

### Desktop: 1024px+

- Four-column default product grid.
- Persistent navigation and search.
- Deliberate whitespace with shorter page sections, not oversized gaps.
- Hover enhancement only; all functionality must remain touch/click accessible.

## 15. Delivery Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation and content audit

- Confirm product/category inventory and business priorities.
- Clean placeholder, duplicate, expired, and inconsistent content.
- Validate contact, shipping, return, payment, and legal information.
- Establish analytics baseline for search, product view, add-to-cart, checkout, and purchase.

### Phase 2 — UX and low-fidelity design

- Finalize sitemap and navigation.
- Create mobile-first wireframes for the homepage, listing, product, cart, and checkout.
- Prototype search, filters, product options, cart, and checkout.
- Test the main journeys with five to eight representative users.

### Phase 3 — Visual system and high-fidelity UI

- Refine brand mark and art direction without losing recognition.
- Build tokens and core components.
- Produce high-fidelity responsive designs and component states.
- Create the product-photography guide and campaign templates.

### Phase 4 — Implementation

- Build the shared storefront shell and component system.
- Implement pages in conversion priority: homepage, listing/search, product, cart, checkout, account/support.
- Connect WooCommerce data and CMS-managed campaigns.
- Add analytics events and structured data.

### Phase 5 — QA and launch

- Test real devices, browsers, slow connections, keyboard, and screen readers.
- Validate checkout, payments, delivery calculations, emails, and order tracking.
- Check metadata, redirects, structured data, and Core Web Vitals.
- Roll out with monitoring and a documented rollback path.

## 16. Definition of Done

- No broken images, placeholder text, expired demo content, or conflicting contact information.
- A new visitor can identify what Torun Mart sells and why it is trustworthy above the fold.
- Search, category browsing, filtering, product selection, cart, and guest checkout work at 320px width and above.
- Product cards and product pages use complete, consistent content and imagery.
- Delivery cost/timing, payment choices, and return conditions are visible before purchase.
- All interactive components have default, hover, focus, active, loading, error, disabled, and success states where applicable.
- Core journeys pass keyboard and screen-reader checks and meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- Core Web Vitals meet the stated performance targets on representative mobile traffic.
- Commerce analytics capture the full funnel without double-counting.

## 17. Success Measures

Compare the redesign with the current baseline using:

- Product discovery rate from homepage
- Search success and zero-result rate
- Product-view-to-add-to-cart rate
- Cart-to-checkout and checkout completion rate
- Mobile conversion rate
- Repeat purchase rate
- Support contacts about delivery, returns, and ordering confusion
- Core Web Vitals and image/error monitoring
